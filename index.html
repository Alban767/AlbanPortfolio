HEAL+IX

The HEAL+ Intelligent eXchange System (HEAL+IX) is engineered to solve these problems — not only in remote, under-served facilities, but across the entire spectrum of Ugandan healthcare delivery. HEAL+IX is a low-cost, AI-driven autonomous medical platform that screens patients, acquires vital signs, dispenses medications rapidly and accurately, automates medical reporting, and connects any patient to a qualified remote physician in real time through a secure telemetry interface.

Critically, HEAL+IX is not a product designed only for rural facilities. Its fast, low-cost automated dispensing, intelligent patient triage, pandemic early-warning capabilities, and automated reporting make it equally valuable in large urban and referral hospitals in Kampala and other cities — where patient volumes overwhelm staff and dispensing queues waste hours of clinical time daily.

The HEAL+IX development is complete with Phase 1 and in the next phase 2, it will be equiped with Robotic Arms to enable it do minor surgeries. The robotic arms will also give remote doctors ability to perform supervised minor surgical procedures from any location, and this will be a foundational platform for robotic telesurgery in low-resource settings
